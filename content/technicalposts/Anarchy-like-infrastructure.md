## What is anarchy ? 
"A situation in which there is no organization and control, especially in society, because there is no effective government"
- [Cambridge dictionary](https://dictionary.cambridge.org/dictionary/english/anarchy)

This is not a political statement but a fact. Most governments or large organisatios have a tendency to creep into our personal lives and try to regulate them. Their role is to break into things that work and call themselves a hero for creating the problems they are solving. This is shown time and time again in history. While it's important to have a governing state it's important to have a clear boundary on where they cannot enter. Equipping yourself with knowledge is the best weapon against these bureaucrats. 

In this blog post we will go in depth in context of what's happening in the area of hosted infrastructure and computer science in general. The goal is show how infrastructure is getting centralised in name of connivence but this is does is the contrary and creates more wasted efforts in unnecessary steps to achieve a simple goal.

Let's look at our day to day activities (For most people): 
- _Communication_ (Emails, Instant messaging etc...)
- _Experiences_ (Photos and videos)
- _Sharing_ (Social media)
- _Entertainment_ (Watching movies)

Ask yourself what tools do you use for the following above. Probably some centralised service that has trapped
you. For example using instagram or WhatsApp for keeping in touch with people far away or literally a few miles away. It's a trapped loop which is hard for people to get out of once trapped in. 

The solution is simple on paper:
- Reduce reliance on them. 
- Improve by using tools which you have full control on. 

**Reduce reliance on them**: If you are on social media filter out the friends you want to keep in touch with
and provide them with your email or phone number (Make sure you have several modes of alternative communication available). This creates discipline and filters the right set of people to keep in touch with. 
The is better as well since you are no longer tied to one tool and you will think of specific details such as the backup policies of your messages, even if your phone is lost from any machine with an internet connection you can restore communication. This makes you more in control and a prepared individual.

**Improve by using tools which you have full control on**: To take this a step further buy your own machines and self host your own mail server (for emails), build your own personal website. You can get multiple cheap 2nd hand computers which you use as personal servers for easily 10 years. Share publicly your pictures and status only through a machine you own. To accomplish this requires an individual to willing to be tech literate. It's far more superior to have this knowledge this creates almost no use for big tech.
